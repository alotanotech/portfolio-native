Designer & Developer

I'm Christopher Aurellyan Alotano Luna, a creative designer and aspiring web developer who enjoys turning ideas into visual and interactive experiences.

My work sits somewhere between graphic design, digital art, and web development. I enjoy experimenting with visual composition, typography, layouts, illustration, and code to create work that feels simple while still having its own character.

Graphic design is where I spend most of my creative energy. I work with raster and vector graphics, digital illustration, interface design, branding, and visual content. I also explore 3D design and interactive experiences as another way to expand the way I approach visual problems.

On the development side, I enjoy building websites and experimenting with the relationship between design and technology. I like taking an idea from a visual concept and gradually turning it into something functional through HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and other web technologies.

I'm still learning, experimenting, and refining my approach. The goal is not simply to make things look good, but to understand why they work and how design, interaction, and technology can support one another.
